What a pass means

The manifest's signature and canonical hash match the run that produced it — so the provider, model, and prompt recorded against this asset are the ones that actually made it.

What a fail means

Not necessarily tampering. An asset re-encoded by a social platform loses its embedded manifest, which is a known limit of embedding rather than evidence of a forged claim.
