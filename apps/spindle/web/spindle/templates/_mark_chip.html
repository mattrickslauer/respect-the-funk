{# ============================================================================= _mark_chip.html — the same mark, at chrome size ============================================================================= `_mark.html` at 20px is a smudge, and it was measured rather than assumed: 64 bars five units wide land on about a third of a device pixel each, the four groove rings collapse into one grey ring, and the disc — the thing that makes it a record — ends up smaller than the halo around it. What renders is an asterisk. So the chrome gets a redrawn mark rather than a scaled one, which is the ordinary answer to this and the reason real marks ship in sizes. The rule the redraw follows: **the disc has to win.** At this size the bars are the first thing to turn into fuzz and the last thing that should be allowed to set the silhouette. So the disc is proportionally much larger than on the hero (r=16 against a 24-unit half-box, where the hero's is 88 against 165), the bars are short enough to read as a halo on it rather than a sun behind it, and three of the four grooves are gone because they were one grey ring at any size that fits in a header. **The 8 bars are the same waveform, resampled, not redrawn.** They come from the `norm()` in `docs/brand/mark.py` — the same three cosine harmonics as the hero, at N=8 with the radii scaled to this box. So the tall bar is at the top and the short one at the bottom on both marks, which is the thing a reader would notice if it were wrong and could not name if it were right. Re-run that formula to change it; do not nudge a rect. Below about 18px even these go muddy, so the console rail hides them — see the `.brand .spindle-chip .rays` rule in `_console.html`. That is the third and last step of the same ladder: 64 bars on the hero, 8 in a header, none in the favicon (`_design.html`) or the rail. Static, deliberately. The hero turns because it is the one place a logo is allowed to be the thing you look at. A header rides above every page in the product, and a header that is always moving is a header nobody can read past. `aria-hidden`, because every call site puts the wordmark next to it. Needs `_design.html` for its tokens — see `_mark.html`'s header for what happens on a surface that does not include it. ============================================================================= #}
