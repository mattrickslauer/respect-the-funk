Somebody was sent a record; the job is to play it in one click and get out of the way. ## Why it extends `_public.html` rather than carrying its own look Because `_design.html`'s header is right. Four palettes drifted apart in this repository before that file existed, and a fifth — written for the one surface most likely to be seen by somebody outside the company — is exactly how a fifth would start. The page a stranger judges the label by should be the page most firmly inside the design system, not least. `_public.html` also already carries `noindex, nofollow`, which matters more here than it does on the sign-in page: these URLs point at commercial masters, and a search engine that indexed one would make a per-recipient link a public one. ## The audio element does not hold a presigned URL `src` is `/listen//audio`, which 302s to a freshly-signed S3 URL per request. Embedding the signature would put it in view-source, in the browser history and in anything that scrapes the page — and it would be stale fifteen minutes later, so a curator who left the tab open would find a dead player. `preload="none"` is deliberate. The redirect is a request that records an event, and a browser that preloaded metadata would log a listen for a page nobody played.
