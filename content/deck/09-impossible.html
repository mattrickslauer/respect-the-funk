spindlesafety, as a property of the data 09 / 15
the promises are not promises

Four things this system is unable to do.

cannot happen
Send the same letter twice
A second approval is a failed write, not a second copy in flight. The duplicate is refused by the shape of the data, before anything reaches a mail server.
cannot happen
Write to someone who said stop
Opting out is final and nothing can undo it — including a well-meaning process that rediscovers the same public address next week.
cannot happen
Mail an address we guessed
A guessed contact is refused outright rather than ranked lower. That was a real bug once: ordering is a preference, and this needed a rule.
cannot happen
Two campaigns, one person
One open conversation per person, per label. Closing it releases them the same instant. A label with thirty campaigns cannot collide with itself.
Not things we are careful about. Things the system has no way to do. the rule lives in the data
