Why a conversation

It is the unit the operator already thinks in, and the one whose cost to us actually scales: research, drafting, and somebody's attention.

Why not a seat

One operator running four hundred conversations is getting four hundred conversations of value. A seat prices the human, not the work.

Why not an email

That pays us for mailing more strangers — the exact incentive this product is built to refuse.
