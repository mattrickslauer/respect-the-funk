ORDER OF THE DAY — WHY THIS ORDER AND NOT SCRIPT ORDER
===============================================================================

Beat 5 is the centrepiece and it is governed by gc.ttlseconds, roughly TEN
HOURS. The send whose ranking gets replayed must still be inside the window at
the moment the screen is captured. Everything else bends around that.

    MORNING     Pre-flight. Then MAKE THE SEND (PF7). The clock starts here.
    MIDDAY      Screen captures S1-S14, beat 5 block first while the window
                is widest.
    AFTERNOON   Camera. The founder reads to picture he has already seen, which
                is why screens come first — the holds and the reads have to
                match, and it is cheaper to re-read a line than to re-capture
                a terminal.
    LATE        B-roll and proof plates. No dialogue, no pressure, and the
                light is better.

Do not shoot camera first. A read paced against an imagined terminal never cuts
to the real one.
