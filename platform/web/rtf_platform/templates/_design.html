{# ============================================================================= _design.html — the shared design system, as an include rather than a file ============================================================================= Four palettes were in this repository before this file: `landing.html` ("a transmitter room at night"), `_console.html` ("instrument panel"), `_public.html` (the sign-in surface), and the operator manual. They were not four *designs* — they were one design typed out four times, drifting. Measured: the manual's dark-mode accent is `#ffb340` and the landing's is `#ffb340`; the manual's carrier is `#3fd6c5` and the landing's is `#35d0c0`; `_public.html`'s accent `#63b3c4` is the console's `--meas`, arrived at independently. The drift was already happening and nobody had a place to stop it. This is that place. ## Why an include and not a stylesheet There is no static file mount in this app and adding one is not free — it is a change to `main.py`, a route, a cache-header decision, and a second thing to get right in Lambda. A Jinja include costs none of that and gives the same single source of truth. The cost is that the tokens ship inline on every page, which for a four-page public surface is a few kB against one fewer round trip. Revisit when the React console lands and genuinely needs a `.css` file it can import; at that point this file becomes the generator of it rather than a second copy. ## The two grounds One token set, two grounds, because the surfaces have genuinely different jobs: `transmitter` — dark, pinned. The landing page and the console. A thing you monitor, at night, for hours. Dark is not a preference here, it is the room. `paper` — light-first, follows the reader's system setting. The manual. A thing you read once, carefully, probably in daylight, quite possibly printed. A surface pins its ground with `data-ground` on . Omit it and you get paper-following-the-system, which is the right default for a document. ## The rule about colour, inherited from _console.html and not negotiable Colour carries meaning and nothing else. There is no decorative hue in this file. If a thing is grey it is because it is fine. The two brand hues — phosphor amber and carrier teal — mark *emphasis* and *liveness* respectively and are not interchangeable. The CockroachDB purple is quarantined at the bottom: it is somebody else's trademark and it is never used for our own UI. ============================================================================= #}
