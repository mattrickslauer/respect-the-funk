The frontier Forager design §4

Not a crawler with a list — a queue of leads, each scored, each remembering the lead that produced it. Score decays with depth, so four hops out starts at a thirteenth of the weight and mostly never runs. A lead with a cadence set comes back on a schedule instead of finishing, which is how routine stream and view polling rides the same table, the same claim query and the same worker as discovery.
