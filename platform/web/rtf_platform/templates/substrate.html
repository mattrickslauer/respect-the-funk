Three kinds of truth get three shapes SCOPE-RESET §2a, PLATFORM-SPEC §2b

Every fact carries how it was obtained, and the three classes are stored differently on purpose — so mixing them is a schema error rather than a discipline failure. A measured BPM lives in a table with no confidence column, because a measurement does not have one. A demographic estimate lives in a table that carries its error bar and sample size, because it needs them. An estimate can never overwrite a measurement, because there is nowhere to put it.

ClassMeansExample
measuredComputed from the artefact itself BPM from the audio; median views from public posts
inferredA model's estimate Audience position; vendor demographic shares
assertedA human stated it, and is accountable Rights, splits, a rate agreed on a call

One edge table, two directions contract design §5

fact_basis records what every belief stands on. Its primary key serves the walk up — why do we believe this? Its secondary index serves the walk down — what rests on this? One table, two indexes, and the second one is what makes ten agents behave as one system.

Provenance Walk up from any claim and you get the chain of evidence, back to the fetched page or the human who said it. The runtime rejects an inferred fact that cites nothing.
Invalidation Retract one fact and a recursive query marks everything standing on it stale — across agents, with no cross-agent code. It marks; it never deletes. A purge destroys exactly the audit trail a label needs when a campaign goes sideways.
Credit Walk down from an outcome and you get what was involved in producing it. Close enough to causal on the fast rungs; deliberately not claimed on the slow one.
