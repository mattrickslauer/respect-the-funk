{# ============================================================================= _logo.html — the Spindle mark: a wooden top, mid-spin ============================================================================= Included, not a file, for the same reason `_design.html` is: there is no static mount in this app. See that file's header for the argument. ## Why a top and not the record `docs/brand/thumbnail.svg` draws a record with a spindle hole at its centre, and that mark is still the repository's social preview. This one is the on-site logo and it reads the other meaning of the word on purpose: a spindle is also the peg a top spins on, and a top is a thing you can *watch working*. A record sitting still says catalogue; a top still turning says the fleet is up. ## How the spin is faked A solid of revolution has no visible motion when it turns — that is the whole problem with animating a cone. Concentric rings, which is what a real turned top is painted with, are rotationally symmetric and would sit dead still no matter how fast it span. So the body carries **vertical staves** instead, and they translate horizontally inside a clip of the silhouette. Two copies of the stave group sit one period apart and the pair slides by exactly one period, so the wrap point never lands in view. Three things are deliberately NOT on the spin animation, because they are what makes it read as a lit object rather than a scrolling texture: - the specular highlight, fixed to the left, where the light is - the shoulder ring, which is geometry, not surface - the silhouette itself The wobble is a second, slower animation on a different element, at a period that does not divide the spin's. Precession in a real top is not in phase with its rotation, and matching them makes it look like a rocking toy. `prefers-reduced-motion` stops all three. What remains is a still top, tilted, with its motion arcs — a logo, which is what it has to be anyway in a PNG. ============================================================================= #}
